## [1.1.0](https://github.com/jesse-mm/nightwatch/compare/v1.0.1...v1.1.0) (2026-10-27)

I've been test-driving the first release for a week now, and a few annoyances came up. This release fixes them.

- **Back from the menu shows the time.** Coming back to the watchface from a menu or app, you're already using the watch, so you probably want to know the time too. It now shows straight away instead of waiting for a flick.
- **The backlight shows the time.** I mostly turn on the backlight with a double tap, not a flick, and most of the time I want to see the time and date then as well. Now whenever the backlight comes on, the time shows too. This works on the new Pebble watches, such as the Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.

Both apply when *Auto hide* is on and the time or date is enabled, and show the time for the number of minutes you set, just like a flick. The time also fades in and out softly now instead of popping up.
